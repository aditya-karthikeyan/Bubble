## Live page: full-screen floating arena, paced live feed

- Why the live run looked wrong: the engine publishes a whole deliberation (candidates, round, merge, lock) within milliseconds, and the page applied it as one burst, so the drift and merge never showed; a late viewer snapped straight to the end state. Verified locally with the dev routes: the socket path itself follows the plan correctly.
- Fix: every event goes through a queue with a dwell after rounds, merges, asks, and lock (gapAfter in use-plan-feed.ts); join history plays as a fast recap; a live backlog over 12 events catches up quickly.
- Redesign: the page is the arena (full viewport, no card). One d3-force simulation stays warm; every node orbits its layout target with a wander radius that shrinks as the crew converges. Glass orbs (radial body, inner glow, specular, rim) via shared SVG gradients; consensus bubble wraps its members' live positions; doers are born at the consensus centre and float out. Positions update imperatively per frame (no React re-render per tick); the tab pauses when hidden; reduced motion settles without animation.
- Ending: once booked, the destination glides to the centre and absorbs the crew one by one (staggered take-off, each bubble shrinks and fades as it nears, the venue swells a little per bubble, the consensus bubble dissolves with them); the doers re-form around the destination.
- Fixed: d3 forceX/forceY cache targets at initialization, so per-frame orbits were ignored. Replaced with a per-tick spring force (`seek`) and refreshed collision radii every tick.
- Ending, per Mahil: nothing sits in a fixed shape. Locked: the crew drifts loosely inside the consensus bubble and the doers float at uneven angles and distances on wide orbits. Booked: the destination absorbs the crew while the doers keep floating. New `complete` phase (booked, and caller, inviter, splitter finished; the watcher never counts): the doers are absorbed too and the plan ends as one bubble, the destination grown, labelled "Plan complete" with venue, time, and party size.
