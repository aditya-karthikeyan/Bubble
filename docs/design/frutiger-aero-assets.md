# Frutiger Aero artwork

The original landscape was generated with the built-in image-generation tool and optimized to a 1536 × 1024 WebP (408,466 bytes). It is used by the signup, QR, and live-plan screens, including reflections inside the live SVG bubbles.

Asset: `apps/web/public/images/aero-world.webp`

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed website background artwork, landscape 1536x1024. Create an exquisite realistic Frutiger Aero 2005 utopian desktop wallpaper: rich azure blue sky, soft billowing white cumulus clouds, radiant sunlight at upper right, lush rolling bright green grassy hills on left and right, a serene turquoise clear lake stretching across the lower half with realistic water ripples, small distant sleek white glass futuristic city on the far right horizon. Three large transparent soap bubbles with realistic refracted inverted landscape and brilliant white reflective crescent highlights float on the left and far right edges. A few small crystal bubbles above the water. Fine vivid fresh grass at bottom corners. Main upper-center and center should be open airy pale blue sky and lake, calm enough for website text. Beautiful glossy hyperreal environmental compositing, photographic grass and water, early 2000s optimistic eco-futurism, fresh cyan blue and vivid lime, high definition, luminous, expansive. No text, no UI, no frames, no logos, no watermark. Wide landscape composition.

## Typography

Righteous for rounded retro display headings and the wordmark; Source Sans 3 for humanist body text, controls, and live bubble labels. Both are loaded with display=swap and local system fallbacks.
